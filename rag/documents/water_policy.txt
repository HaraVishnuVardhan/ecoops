EcoOps 2.0 Demo Campus Water Conservation Policy

Document type: Synthetic hackathon demonstration policy.

1. Unusual increases in water consumption should be investigated before intervention.

2. Facilities teams should check for possible leaks, abnormal usage patterns, occupancy changes, and campus events.

3. Water sensor readings should be validated when a sensor has recently been installed, replaced, recalibrated, or repaired.

4. Historical water consumption should be compared with current readings to determine whether the pattern is consistent with previous operating conditions.

5. Automatic operational changes should not be made when the cause of an anomaly is uncertain.

6. Suspected infrastructure failures, persistent unexplained increases, or high-risk water anomalies should be escalated to the campus facilities team.

7. Recommendations should include the evidence and policy guidance used to produce them.
