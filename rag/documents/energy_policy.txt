EcoOps 2.0 Demo Campus Energy Policy

Document type: Synthetic hackathon demonstration policy.

1. Energy anomalies above normal operating baselines should be investigated before operational changes are made.

2. Facilities teams should review HVAC schedules, equipment operating hours, occupancy patterns, and recent campus events when abnormal energy consumption is detected.

3. Historical consumption patterns should be compared with the current reading to determine whether the anomaly is unusual for the location and time period.

4. Sensor readings should be validated when the observed increase is inconsistent with expected operational conditions.

5. Automatic optimization should not be applied when there is evidence of equipment malfunction, sensor uncertainty, or an extreme unexplained consumption increase.

6. High-risk or unexplained energy anomalies should be escalated to the campus facilities team for human review.

7. Any operational change should be traceable to the anomaly, supporting evidence, and applicable policy guidance.
