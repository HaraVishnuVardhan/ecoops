EcoOps 2.0 Demo Campus Waste Management Policy

Document type: Synthetic hackathon demonstration policy.

1. Unexpected increases in campus waste generation should be investigated before corrective action.

2. Waste generation should be compared with occupancy, events, academic schedules, and historical patterns.

3. Data quality should be checked when waste measurements differ substantially from expected values.

4. Campus events may cause temporary increases in waste generation and should be considered when interpreting anomalies.

5. Automatic operational changes should not be recommended when the cause of an increase cannot be established with sufficient confidence.

6. Persistent or unexplained waste anomalies should be reviewed by the responsible campus operations team.

7. Recommendations should clearly identify the evidence and policy guidance supporting the proposed action.
